Avalanche Bridge.

The Avalanche Bridge moves ETH, USDC and other tokens from Ethereum to the Avalanche C-Chain, where you pay gas in AVAX and use them across the Avalanche DeFi ecosystem. You review the route, the fee and the amount received before you sign, and your funds stay in your own wallet the whole time.

How to use the Avalanche Bridge

Connect a wallet, choose the asset and amount, and confirm. To use the Avalanche Bridge you select the token on Ethereum, review the live route, the fee and the amount that will arrive on the Avalanche C-Chain, then approve and sign. The assets appear on Avalanche once the source transaction has enough confirmations, and nothing moves until you sign.

Avalanche Bridge fees and time

The cost of the Avalanche Bridge is the Ethereum gas to send plus any routing or protocol fee; receiving on Avalanche is cheap because AVAX gas is low. Timing depends on Ethereum confirmations and network load — usually minutes. The quote shows the amount received before you commit, so there are no surprises at signing.

What you can bridge to Avalanche

You can bridge ETH, USDC, USDT, WBTC and other liquid assets to the Avalanche C-Chain, where they work across Avalanche DeFi — DEXs, lending and liquidity pools. The exact list depends on supported routes at the time; the interface shows which assets are reachable and the expected amount for each.

Is the Avalanche Bridge safe to use?

A self-custody bridge keeps your keys in your wallet. To use the Avalanche Bridge safely, confirm the destination token's contract address, the amount received and the exact token approval you grant, and prefer a limited approval over an unlimited one. Keep a little AVAX for gas on the destination, and treat any request for a seed phrase as fraud.

Native route vs third-party Avalanche bridges

The native Avalanche Bridge moves major assets directly between Ethereum and the C-Chain. Third-party bridges can reach more chains or assets but add another contract and fee. Judge any route by the net amount received after gas and fees, not the headline rate, and prefer well-audited routes with deep liquidity.
Do I need AVAX to use the Avalanche Bridge?
You pay Ethereum gas to send, and you need a small amount of AVAX on the Avalanche C-Chain to move your assets once they arrive.
How long does the Avalanche Bridge take?
Usually minutes, after your Ethereum transaction gets enough confirmations. Congested periods take longer and cost more gas on the source side.
Which assets can I bridge to Avalanche?
ETH, USDC, USDT, WBTC and other liquid tokens, depending on supported routes. The interface shows what is reachable and the amount received.
Do I need an account to use the Avalanche Bridge?
No. You connect a self-custody wallet and bridge on-chain, with no sign-up and no custody of your funds.
What happens if my bridge transfer fails?
A failed source transaction reverts and you keep your assets, though the gas for the attempt is spent. Pending transfers usually clear once the network is less busy.
